Hi, I'm Pradnya

Senior UX/UI and Product Designer with 10+ years of design experience, including 7+ years designing web and mobile products across fintech, hospitality, entertainment, travel, e-commerce and SaaS.

I've designed digital experiences for Global Village Dubai and Dubai Holding Entertainment, and I own the end-to-end design process, from research and user flows to design systems and developer handoff. I specialise in accessible, bilingual (Arabic RTL / English) and multi-currency experiences. I use AI tools such as Figma Make, Claude and ChatGPT to speed up research, prototyping and documentation.
